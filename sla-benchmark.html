Dual-Track Accountability Model

The 90-day cycle separates what the team controls from what depends on brand response. Team performance is measured against the 32-day internal SLA. External delays are escalation-managed, not penalised.

Internal SLA

32 days

Fully within Opptra team control

  • AI-enabled brand scoring & qualification (1d)
  • Outreach execution & follow-up cadence (2d)
  • LOB / CEO approval gates via async Slack (4d total across 4 gates)
  • GTM proposal with templatised P&L + Claude (3d)
  • BizFin review in parallel (1d)
  • Contract drafting with template agreements (5d)
  • Internal legal review & chairman pre-read (7d)
  • DocuSign routing & signing logistics (5d)

External SLA

58 days

Brand-dependent, escalation-managed

  • Brand response to outreach (10d, escalate at day 7)
  • Brand intent confirmation across 3 TZs (8d, escalate at day 5)
  • Kickstart call scheduling & procurement alignment (7d)
  • Terms negotiation & legal redlines (32d — multi-geo licensing)
  • Brand-side signing & execution (9d, DocuSign reminders at 3/5/7d)

Stage-by-Stage Rationale

Wishlist → Lead (13 days)

Steps 1–3. AI scoring (Claude skill) compresses connect info and scoring from 7d to 1d INT. Brand response to initial outreach = 10d external dependency with auto-escalation at day 7 EXT. Lead qualification is same-day VP/Deal Owner decision INT 1d. LOB approval INT 1d.

Lead → MQL (14 days)

Steps 4–7. Outreach execution automated INT 1d. Brand intent confirmation across GCC/India/SEA timezones — 8d with escalation at day 5 EXT 8d. LOB + CEO approval at 1d each with async Slack INT 2d. Assessment prep INT 3d.

MQL → SQL (15 days)

Steps 8–12. Kickstart call (5d) and procurement costs (5d) run in parallel — net 5d EXT. Assessment & GTM framework INT 5d. BizFin parallel review INT 1d. LOB + CEO approval 1d each INT 2d. Brand-side procurement alignment EXT 2d.

SQL → Opportunity (46 days)

Steps 13–20. GTM proposal reduced from 15d to 3d via templatised P&L + Claude INT. Terms alignment — the #1 bottleneck per Samarth — 10d with mid-point check EXT. Chairman approval with pre-read pack + weekly board slot INT 7d. Draft agreement with templates INT 5d. Contract negotiation expanded to 14d — multi-geo exclusive licensing with royalty structures requires 2–4 rounds of legal redlines EXT. Brand-side review & countersign prep EXT 8d.

Opportunity → Signed (14 days)

Steps 19–20. Final internal sign-off & DocuSign routing INT 5d. Brand-side execution & countersigning with DocuSign reminders at day 3, 5, 7 EXT 9d.

Process improvement flags: CEO currently approves at 4 gates — consider delegating MQL gate to LOB Head for deals under $2M Y1. Parallelise steps 8+9 (kickstart + procurement) and 14+15 (terms + BizFin). Auto-escalation triggers at 75% of each SLA threshold via Slack.

Industry Benchmarks

The 90-day target is calibrated against fashion/beauty distribution and franchise partnership deal cycles, not generic B2B SaaS. Sources below reflect the specific dynamics of exclusive territory licensing, franchise agreements, and multi-geo retail partnerships.

Fashion Law Institute
4–8 months
Exclusive territory licensing agreements in fashion. Includes brand due diligence, territory negotiation, royalty structure, and legal review. Complex multi-territory deals extend to 12+ months.
Franchise Times
6–12 months
Master franchise agreements from LOI to signing. Includes territory rights, operational standards, supply chain setup. International master franchise deals average 9+ months.
WGSN / McKinsey
90–120 days
Best-in-class fast-track brand partnerships in fashion. Top-quartile operators with standardised contracts, AI-assisted due diligence, and streamlined approval chains achieve sub-100-day cycles.
Altagamma Foundation
6–18 months
Luxury brand licensing deals across Europe, Middle East, and Asia. Premium/luxury segments take longer due to brand protection clauses, exclusivity terms, and multi-stakeholder governance.
Opptra Internal (SL_BD_Master)
120 days (team proposal)
Internal team's original 20-step process estimate across the full pipeline. Suphasak's target: 62 days. Revised compromise at 90 days with AI compression on internal stages and realistic external buffers.
Opptra's 90-day target = top-quartile performance — achievable via AI-compressed internal stages (brand scoring, GTM proposals, approvals) while maintaining realistic buffers for external brand dependencies and multi-geo legal complexity.

Automation Architecture

The BD Command Center uses a detect → notify → approve → update workflow. Claude scans connected systems daily, matches events to pipeline brands, and posts findings to Slack. Humans stay in control of every stage transition.

Fully Auto-Detectable (3)

Gmail · GCal · Drive
  • Gmail: Brand reply detection (Wishlist→Lead), deck prep emails (MQL→SQL)
  • GCal: External attendee meetings (Lead→MQL) — live and tested
  • Drive: Signed contract PDFs (Opp→Signed)

Semi-Auto / Human Trigger (2)

Slack · LinkedIn
  • Slack: CEO approval via reaction (SQL→Opportunity)
  • LinkedIn: No API — BD logs via Slack command or Notion button
  • In-person meetings: Manual note in Notion page body

Notion SLA Formula

Paste this formula into the SLA Status property in the BD Pipeline Notion database. The API doesn't support lets(), so this must be pasted manually in Notion UI (takes ~2 minutes).

lets( days, dateBetween(now(), prop("Stage Entry Date"), "days"), sla, if(prop("Status") == "Lead", 13, if(prop("Status") == "MQL", 14, if(prop("Status") == "SQL", 15, if(prop("Status") == "Opportunity", 46, if(prop("Status") == "Signed", 14, 30))))), ratio, days / sla, if( prop("Status") == "Active" or prop("Status") == "Hold" or prop("Status") == "Rejected" or prop("Status") == "Wishlist" or empty(prop("Stage Entry Date")), "", if(ratio >= 1, "🚨 Breached", if(ratio >= 0.75, "⚠️ Warning", "✅ On Track")) ) )
